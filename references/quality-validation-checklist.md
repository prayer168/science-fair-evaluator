# Science Fair Output Quality Validation Checklist

Run this checklist after drafting a full evaluation or prework report. Repair failures before final delivery.

## A. Evidence Integrity

- [ ] Current or changeable facts were verified.
- [ ] Prior-work search includes Taiwan science-fair records and international academic/technical sources when available.
- [ ] Search used synonyms, mechanism terms, materials, measurements, and application terms—not only the exact title keyword.
- [ ] Source types are distinguished: academic, official, technical, educational, student project.
- [ ] No citation, result, DOI, author, year, URL, or novelty claim was invented.
- [ ] Novelty claims are calibrated to the actual search boundary.
- [ ] Assumptions and inferences are labeled.

## B. Novelty & Overlap Gate

- [ ] A prior-work matrix is present.
- [ ] The strongest 3–8 comparable works are identified.
- [ ] The union of what is already known is summarized.
- [ ] Overlap level O0–O4 is assigned with reasons.
- [ ] Novelty gate N0–N3 is assigned with reasons.
- [ ] The research gap is specific and evidence-based.
- [ ] A do-not-repeat list identifies saturated variables/methods.
- [ ] The exact contribution type is named.
- [ ] O3/O4 directions were redesigned or rejected before Phase 2.
- [ ] Technology (AI, sensors, ESP32, 3D printing, dashboards, heatmaps) is not treated as novelty by itself.

## C. Alignment Chain

Verify this chain for every major item:

`題目 → 研究問題 → 研究目的 → 實驗 → 應變變因 → 測量方法 → 紀錄表 → 圖表 → 判讀`

- [ ] Every research purpose has corresponding evidence.
- [ ] Every experiment supports a listed purpose.
- [ ] At least one purpose directly tests the selected research gap.
- [ ] Every dependent variable has a unit or operational definition.
- [ ] Every record-table column is used in the analysis plan.
- [ ] Every proposed graph can be built from the record table.
- [ ] The conclusion criteria can answer the research question.

## D. Experimental Quality

- [ ] A valid control or baseline is present.
- [ ] Manipulated variables are not unintentionally confounded.
- [ ] Controlled variables are practical, not merely listed.
- [ ] Sample size and repeat count are stated and justified.
- [ ] Pilot testing and calibration are included where needed.
- [ ] Measurement timing, frequency, resolution, and units are stated.
- [ ] Randomization, order effects, and environmental drift are considered.
- [ ] Error sources have concrete control methods.
- [ ] Stopping, exclusion, and redo rules are defined before data collection.
- [ ] Each experiment states how it differs from the closest prior work.

## E. Engineering / Optimization Projects

When applicable:

- [ ] A baseline prototype or reference condition is defined.
- [ ] Objective metrics are defined before testing.
- [ ] Trade-offs between metrics are considered.
- [ ] Robustness/stability is tested across more than one condition when claimed.
- [ ] External or real-world validation is planned.
- [ ] Success and failure criteria are defined before data collection.

## F. Elementary-School Appropriateness

- [ ] Students can explain the core mechanism in age-appropriate language.
- [ ] Students can perform the central measurements themselves.
- [ ] Adult-only tasks are limited and clearly labeled.
- [ ] Mathematical and statistical methods are explainable by students.
- [ ] Student decisions, revisions, and ownership are visible.
- [ ] The project can be completed within the available schedule.

## G. Safety, Ethics, and Environment

- [ ] Hazards are identified at the step where they occur.
- [ ] PPE, adult supervision, emergency response, storage, and disposal are specified.
- [ ] Safer substitutes are provided for avoidable hazards.
- [ ] Human data, privacy, animal welfare, ecological impact, and sampling permission are addressed.
- [ ] No unsupported health or environmental benefit claim is made.

## H. Feasibility and Logistics

- [ ] Material quantities and specifications are sufficient for repeats.
- [ ] Cost estimate includes consumables and replacements.
- [ ] Purchase keywords or likely sources are provided.
- [ ] Seasonality, sample supply, weather, and equipment availability are considered.
- [ ] A fallback exists for the highest-risk dependency.
- [ ] Schedule includes literature/overlap review, pilot, formal trials, redesign, validation, analysis, and presentation.

## I. Data Analysis

- [ ] Raw data are preserved.
- [ ] Missing, invalid, and outlier data rules are explicit.
- [ ] Mean alone is not used when variation is important.
- [ ] Normalization is used when starting conditions differ.
- [ ] Graph axes, units, and comparison groups are clear.
- [ ] Proposed statistics match the sample and question.
- [ ] Alternative explanations and limitations are included.
- [ ] Criteria state what evidence would weaken the prediction or fail to improve the baseline.

## J. 18-Field Architecture Consistency

For each experiment verify:

1. research purpose;
2. experiment ID/name;
3. core question;
4. mechanism;
5. manipulated variable;
6. dependent variable;
7. controlled variables;
8. control/baseline;
9. experimental groups;
10. sample/repeats;
11. materials/equipment;
12. calibration/pilot;
13. procedure;
14. raw-data fields/units;
15. analysis/graphs;
16. invalid/stop/redo rules;
17. difference from prior work/research gap;
18. success criterion/next step.

- [ ] All 18 fields are complete or explicitly marked not applicable with justification.

## K. Stage Gates

- [ ] **G0** Prior-work & novelty passed or has explicit conditional acceptance criteria.
- [ ] **G1** Research alignment passed.
- [ ] **G2** Apparatus & logistics passed.
- [ ] **G3** Pilot readiness passed or pilot is the clearly defined next action.

Formal trials must not be described as ready to begin if G0–G3 are unresolved.

## L. Report Consistency

- [ ] One primary title is used consistently.
- [ ] Terminology, units, group names, and experiment numbers are consistent.
- [ ] The report does not present expected results as completed findings.
- [ ] In-text citations and APA references match.
- [ ] The reference-section heading is `參考文獻`.
- [ ] No section contains contradictory sample sizes, durations, or material quantities.
- [ ] No Discussion or Conclusion section is included in a prework report.

## Final Verdict

Classify the deliverable:

- **Ready**: no hard failures; only cosmetic improvements remain.
- **Conditionally ready**: one or more fixable evidence, logistics, pilot, or alignment gaps remain.
- **Not ready**: safety, testability, citation integrity, student-ownership, or novelty gate fails.

End complete reports with a one-line validation note stating the verdict, the most important remaining uncertainty, and the first pilot-test requirement.

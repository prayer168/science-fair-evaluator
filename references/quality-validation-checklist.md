# Science Fair Output Quality Validation Checklist

Run this checklist after drafting a full evaluation or prework report. Repair failures before final delivery.

## A. Evidence Integrity

- [ ] Current or changeable facts were verified.
- [ ] Prior-work search includes both domestic and international sources when available.
- [ ] Source types are distinguished: academic, official, technical, educational, student project.
- [ ] No citation, result, DOI, author, year, or URL was invented.
- [ ] Novelty claims are calibrated to the actual search boundary.
- [ ] Assumptions and inferences are labeled.

## B. Alignment Chain

Verify this chain for every major item:

`題目 → 研究問題 → 研究目的 → 實驗 → 應變變因 → 測量方法 → 紀錄表 → 圖表 → 判讀`

- [ ] Every research purpose has corresponding evidence.
- [ ] Every experiment supports a listed purpose.
- [ ] Every dependent variable has a unit or operational definition.
- [ ] Every record-table column is used in the analysis plan.
- [ ] Every proposed graph can be built from the record table.
- [ ] The conclusion criteria can answer the research question.

## C. Experimental Quality

- [ ] A valid control or baseline is present.
- [ ] Manipulated variables are not unintentionally confounded.
- [ ] Controlled variables are practical, not merely listed.
- [ ] Sample size and repeat count are stated and justified.
- [ ] Pilot testing and calibration are included where needed.
- [ ] Measurement timing, frequency, resolution, and units are stated.
- [ ] Randomization, order effects, and environmental drift are considered.
- [ ] Error sources have concrete control methods.
- [ ] Stopping or exclusion rules are defined before data collection.

## D. Elementary-School Appropriateness

- [ ] Students can explain the core mechanism in age-appropriate language.
- [ ] Students can perform the central measurements themselves.
- [ ] Adult-only tasks are limited and clearly labeled.
- [ ] Mathematical and statistical methods are explainable by students.
- [ ] Student decisions, revisions, and ownership are visible.
- [ ] The project can be completed within the available schedule.

## E. Safety, Ethics, and Environment

- [ ] Hazards are identified at the step where they occur.
- [ ] PPE, adult supervision, emergency response, storage, and disposal are specified.
- [ ] Safer substitutes are provided for avoidable hazards.
- [ ] Human data, privacy, animal welfare, ecological impact, and sampling permission are addressed.
- [ ] No unsupported health or environmental benefit claim is made.

## F. Feasibility and Logistics

- [ ] Material quantities and specifications are sufficient for repeats.
- [ ] Cost estimate includes consumables and replacements.
- [ ] Purchase keywords or likely sources are provided.
- [ ] Seasonality, sample supply, weather, and equipment availability are considered.
- [ ] A fallback exists for the highest-risk dependency.
- [ ] Schedule includes pilot, formal trials, redesign, analysis, and presentation.

## G. Research Evolution and Novelty

- [ ] The work is more than a known demonstration.
- [ ] The contribution type is named: mechanism, variable, method, context, measurement, analysis, design, or validation.
- [ ] Technology contributes to evidence quality rather than decoration.
- [ ] The topic is narrow enough to investigate rigorously.
- [ ] The claimed research gap is supported by prior-work comparison.

## H. Data Analysis

- [ ] Raw data are preserved.
- [ ] Missing, invalid, and outlier data rules are explicit.
- [ ] Mean alone is not used when variation is important.
- [ ] Normalization is used when starting conditions differ.
- [ ] Graph axes, units, and comparison groups are clear.
- [ ] Proposed statistics match the sample and question.
- [ ] Alternative explanations and limitations are included.
- [ ] Criteria state what evidence would weaken the prediction.

## I. Report Consistency

- [ ] One primary title is used consistently.
- [ ] Terminology, units, group names, and experiment numbers are consistent.
- [ ] The report does not present expected results as completed findings.
- [ ] In-text citations and APA references match.
- [ ] The reference-section heading is `參考文獻`.
- [ ] No section contains contradictory sample sizes, durations, or material quantities.

## Final Verdict

Classify the deliverable:

- **Ready**: no hard failures; only cosmetic improvements remain.
- **Conditionally ready**: one or more fixable evidence, logistics, or alignment gaps remain.
- **Not ready**: safety, testability, citation integrity, or student-ownership gate fails.

End complete reports with a one-line validation note stating the verdict and the most important remaining uncertainty.
